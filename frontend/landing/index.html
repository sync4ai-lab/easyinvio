A few things worth knowing

Good questions.
Clear answers.

The details that make a shipping workflow dependable.

How does UK address verification work?

When configured, EasyInvio uses UK PAF address lookup to help your team select a verified address. Address lookup is a workspace service. A valid postcode alone does not guarantee an address or service is deliverable.

Can I bring my own carrier contracts?

You can connect supported carrier accounts, subject to the carrier’s API access and your agreement. Direct carrier and aggregator adapters have different capabilities. Contact us to confirm your contracts or discuss eligible platform-connected services.

How do WhatsApp QR labels and returns work?

For eligible paperless services, EasyInvio can deliver the carrier-issued drop-off instructions through WhatsApp after authorization and any required payment. QR availability depends on the carrier and return service. Some documents require a signed-in workspace; a return request does not authorize a refund.

How is my prepaid wallet protected?

Stripe handles top-up checkout. EasyInvio records wallet movements and reservations in its ledger, with database locks and role checks around concurrent operations. A top-up is credited after server confirmation; a browser success page cannot credit your wallet.

Are tracking updates and notifications free?

Core tracking visibility is included in the base framework. Proactive WhatsApp notifications depend on recipient consent, approved templates and enabled delivery services. Messaging and other third-party usage can incur charges; they are separate from the free base framework.
