Pricing & billing

A clear answer
before you commit.

Understand the software plan, your postage and the payment controls.

What happens if I exceed 2,000 parcels on Velocity?

Monthly included allowances are 150 parcels on Starter, 2,000 on Velocity and 4,000 on Command. Published overage rates are £0.15, £0.05 and £0.02 respectively, excluding applicable VAT and postage. Automatic overage collection is not enabled; Starter currently stops at 150 parcels. Upgrade or contact us for approved extra capacity. Higher Command volumes can be agreed with our team. Selecting a plan does not automatically charge your account.

Do you charge extra for parcel tracking?

Core tracking visibility and secure customer tracking links are included in the software plan. Carrier events and configured refresh determine how current the information is. Optional proactive WhatsApp messages and other third-party usage can incur separate charges.

How does the prepaid Stripe wallet work?

Stripe handles the top-up checkout. Your wallet is credited only after verified payment confirmation. An authorized booking reserves the selected amount, captures it when the label is confirmed, or releases it after a definitive failure. An uncertain carrier outcome is reconciled before further action. Wallet funds and software subscription fees are separate.

Can I connect my own Royal Mail or Evri account?

Command includes bring-your-own-contract setup for supported carrier integrations, subject to your carrier agreement, API credentials and platform approval. Available services differ by carrier and account; owning a contract alone does not activate API access or guarantee a particular discount.

Can I switch plans or cancel?

The workspace Owner can review plan changes in Wallet → Plan, billing & statements where enabled, or contact support. Upgrades require payment of the remaining-period price difference; downgrades take effect at the end of the paid period. Cancellation requires 30 days’ notice. Annual plans are paid upfront: £240 for Velocity or £720 for Command. If you cancel an annual plan early, the annual discount is removed: elapsed service and the 30-day notice are calculated at standard monthly prices. Any remaining verified payment is refunded to the original payment source after service ends, with the corresponding original tax adjustment. If the calculation leaves an additional balance, it is shown before confirmation. Review your exact dates and amounts before confirming.
