# Understand roles and invite your team

## EasyInvio staff and customer businesses

### EasyInvio Admin (SUPER_ADMIN)
The platform owner has the consolidated client, shipment, payment and support overview. This role comes from server configuration, never a customer invitation. Global pricing, carrier credentials, security configuration and staff privileges remain restricted to the platform owner. Deleting the application or its infrastructure is not an application role feature.

### EasyInvio Agent
An Agent is an EasyInvio support employee, not an employee or owner of a customer business. Agents can inspect consolidated clients, shipments, payments and support, onboard a new client, search for a client and start audited client assistance. Agents cannot change global carrier credentials, platform pricing policy or staff privileges. Disabling the staff grant blocks subsequent access and renewal.

### Assist a client
Admin and Agent select the client and provide a support reason before entering the client's workspace. The interface shows the selected company and a return-to-platform option. Assistance is limited to 30 minutes and the current sign-in lifetime. Work is performed with that client's Owner permissions; the actual staff identity and requested actions are audited. Assistance does not change the customer's password or create active employee membership. An ended or expired assistance session must be reopened before further operations.

## Customer workspace roles

### Owner
An Owner runs one customer business. They can prepare, quote, book and track shipments, arrange returns, raise support cases, manage store integrations, saved addresses, contacts, products and workspace notification preferences, fund the wallet and manage finance. The Owner manages company details and invites, disables or changes Dispatcher and Accountant users. Team invitations cannot create another Owner, a tenant Admin, an EasyInvio Admin or an Agent. The same restrictions apply when staff assist a client.

### Dispatcher
A Dispatcher prepares, imports, quotes, books, manifests, prints and tracks shipments, handles returns and operational support, manages addresses, contacts, products and store integrations. The operational overview covers their company's shipments. They can purchase a reviewed label basket by card and arrange customer-paid return checkout; this permission is bound to the exact saved basket, price, company and requesting user. They cannot make arbitrary wallet top-ups, manage company/team settings, change billing configuration or see detailed financial margins and wholesale costs. Successful labels may spend existing company wallet credit. Failed or partial card bookings retain unused credit for reconciliation; they never permit duplicate purchases.

### Accountant
An Accountant sees the company's financial overview, wallet, billing, payments, invoices and shipment history. They can perform supported finance actions, including funding the company wallet. They cannot create quotes, book labels, create returns, modify products or store integrations, change company settings or manage users. Shipment history is visible for financial review. Payment corrections, refunds and settlements still follow their verified provider and ledger workflows; a role cannot manufacture a payment or refund.

### Legacy company ADMIN
Older company ADMIN memberships remain supported for existing shipping and finance workflows. They are not platform administrators and cannot manage the team under the current Owner-only policy. New company invitations offer only Dispatcher and Accountant. Ask the platform owner to review an existing legacy membership rather than guessing from its name.

## Add a colleague

### Invite Dispatcher or Accountant
Open Settings as the business Owner, choose the team/company section, enter the colleague's name and work email, and choose Dispatcher or Accountant. Send the invitation. The colleague verifies their email and chooses a password through the invitation flow. Owners cannot invite another Owner or platform staff member.

### Missing menus or access problems
Sign out and back in after a role change. Ask the business Owner to check your active membership. An Agent should see the platform console and select a client through assistance, not sign in as an employee of a sample business. Use Forgot password for password recovery. Never share passwords, card details, carrier keys or access tokens with support.
